How this picture is constructed

We first compute the attracting eigenlines of all cyclically reduced words through length three. The default sampler explores their orbits, pruning already sampled projective cells: if p attracts M, then gp attracts gMg⁻¹. Random conjugates and reduced words are also available.

The sphere is S² ⊂ ℝ³, the double cover of ℝℙ². Both p and −p are drawn, with the rear hemisphere translucent. Dragging changes the camera, not the representation.

Changing the surface, spin or differential rebuilds the mesh, solves the harmonic metric on both sheets, and computes new holonomies. All six odd and ten even genus-two spin structures are included. For even spins, the metric need not be deck-invariant.

For an exact Anosov representation these attracting fixed points belong to its limit curve and are dense. Numerical holonomies, finite sampling and chart interpolation introduce error. If the relation error is large, increase the resolution before interpreting the cloud.

Only points are drawn, without a filled curve interior or edges across infinity. Pending parameter edits do not alter the displayed representation until recomputation succeeds.
