Research

Geometry, topology and dynamics of surface group representations.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics “Giuseppe Peano” at the University of Turin. I work in the ERC project GENERATE, led by Andrea Seppi. I completed my PhD at Heidelberg University under the supervision of Beatrice Pozzetti.

  • Anosov representations
  • Higher Teichmüller theory
  • Symmetric spaces
  • Geometric structures
